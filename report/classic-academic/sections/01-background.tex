\section{\Localized{Background}{背景}}
\label{sec:background}

\Localized{Scientific reports often need a shorter hierarchy than a dissertation while retaining rigorous typography. This template uses the same visual tokens, fonts, colours, captions, and scientific components as the thesis template, while its document model is based on the article class and a continuous section hierarchy.}{科学报告通常需要比学位论文更紧凑的层级，同时保持严谨排版。本模板与学位论文使用相同的视觉标记、字体、配色、题注和科学组件，但文档模型采用 article 类和连续的节层级。}
